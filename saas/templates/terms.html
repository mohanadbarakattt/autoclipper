Terms & takedowns

Draft for the beta. A reviewed version comes before paid plans open.

Your responsibility

Virlo Studio is an editing tool. You may only clip streams you own or have the right to clip (your own channel, the streamer's permission, or a clipping campaign that allows it). You are responsible for what you post. Credit the streamer; captions do this by default.

Not allowed

Clipping a streamer who has asked not to be clipped, fake engagement or view bots, and anything illegal.

Streamers: opt out or take a clip down

If you're a streamer and don't want to be clipped on Virlo Studio, or want a clip removed, email takedown@virlo.studio with your channel link (and the clip, if any). We block opted-out channels for every user and remove reported clips within 48 hours. Accounts with three valid notices are closed.

Your data

We store your email, channels and clips. Clips are deleted after your plan's history window. You can delete your account and everything in it from the Account page at any time.
